# Lanternleaf main characters

**Status:** Working

Lanternleaf currently has four main characters. Their names, species, pronouns,
roles, accessories, and accepted character sheets below are the current source
of truth. Character eras and some village origins remain open.

## Current cast

| Character | Species | Pronouns | Home | Current story function | Accepted sheet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dia** | Fox | she/her | Mossgrove | Starting lead; navigator and mapmaker | [`dia.png`](dia.png) |
| **Zippy** | Bee | he/him | Open; begins beside Dia | Starting lead; aerial scout and messenger | [`zippy.png`](zippy.png) |
| **Pebble** | Tortoise | he/him | Open | Builder; makes routes practically passable | [`pebble.png`](pebble.png) |
| **Kavi** | Elephant | she/her | Sunbank | Listener and storykeeper; carries perspectives between villages | [`kavi.png`](kavi.png) |

Dia and Zippy form the opening pair. Pebble joins before Kavi. Kavi is the
fourth and latest addition to the traveling group.

Village origins may shape what a character knows without determining their
personality or occupation. Main and recurring characters do not need to be
distributed evenly among the four villages.

## Design restraint

Each lead has one strong silhouette and, at most, one persistent role-bearing
accessory. Scene-specific tools and props appear only when the story needs them.

The accepted sheets linked above define current visual identity:

- Dia carries her mapmaking satchel and wears her short olive cloak.
- Zippy consistently wears his wooden compass on one continuous sling.
- Pebble wears his builder's belt with its wooden cord bobbin.
- Kavi wears one five-bead Sunbank clay bracelet on her left wrist. She is a
  fully upright young elephant with an oversized head, short compact body, and
  lighter blue-grey belly. She is approximately 1.3 times Dia's standing height
  in shared scenes.

Do not transfer anatomy, face construction, colors, clothing, or accessories
between characters.
