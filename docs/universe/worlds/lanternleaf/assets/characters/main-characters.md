# Lanternleaf main characters

**Status:** Working

Lanternleaf currently has four main characters. Their names, species, pronouns,
and the origins listed below are established working decisions. Visual designs,
character eras, and unresolved story functions remain open.

## Current cast

| Character | Species | Pronouns | Home | Current story function |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nibbles** | Fox | she/her | Mossgrove | Starting lead; navigator and mapmaker |
| **Zippy** | Bee | he/him | Open; begins the story alongside Nibbles | Starting lead; aerial scout and messenger |
| **Pickle** | Tortoise | he/him | Open | Main traveler; exact function open |
| **Pepper** | Snow wolf | she/her | Kite Hill | Main traveler; exact function open |

Nibbles and Zippy form the opening pair. Pickle and Pepper join the traveling
group, but their joining order has not been set.

Village origins may shape what a character knows without determining their
personality or occupation. Main and recurring characters do not need to be
distributed evenly among the four villages.

## Design restraint

Each lead will receive one strong silhouette and, at most, one persistent
role-bearing accessory. Scene-specific tools and props should appear only when
the story needs them.

No current image defines the four characters' visual identity. In particular,
`docs/universe/fern-and-pip.png` remains useful only for Mossgrove setting
vocabulary, character-to-world scale, and inhabited scene layering.
