# Lanternleaf Future Scene Prompt Template

Use the supplied Lanternleaf character references:

* **Image A = [Primary character]**
* **Image B = [Supporting character]**

Treat each reference independently as the authoritative source for that character’s identity.
Do **not** blend facial traits, anatomy, markings, accessories, or proportions between them.

Create a **4:3 children’s storybook illustration** on a **perfectly solid #FFFFFF matte** with a **floating watercolor vignette** and generous irregular white space.

Let the illustration **fade out early in low-information areas**, especially:

* upper background
* sparse outer edges
* around hovering / secondary characters when detail is not needed

The edges should feel **airy, soft, and abstract**, not densely filled.

## Style

Use a **hand-painted watercolor-and-ink storybook style**.

### Watercolor

Use **smooth, broad, contiguous watercolor fields with low-frequency tonal variation**.

Paint major forms as:

* broad transparent washes
* calm continuous color regions
* gentle large-scale transitions
* soft unified shapes

Treat fur, clothing, leaves, roots, wings, ground, and props as **large painted masses first**.

Avoid:

* mottled texture
* patchy brush texture
* repeated tiny brush marks
* stippling
* dry-brush fill texture
* noisy moss texture
* furry internal texture strokes
* repeated foliage dabs
* many tiny value changes

### Linework

Use **hand-drawn storybook contour lines** that are:

* slightly irregular
* gently wobbly
* pressure-varied
* softly tapered
* occasionally lightly broken
* locally tinted to the form, never universal black

Use longer confident strokes around major forms, with only a few small flicks where needed.

Examples of local contour color:

* deeper orange around orange fur
* warm beige around cream fur
* deeper green around leaves
* deeper brown around wood / roots / leather
* honey-ochre around yellow forms
* muted lavender-brown around wings

Avoid:

* vector-clean outlines
* uniform line thickness
* mechanical tracing
* polished digital contouring
* black outline everywhere

## Avoid

Avoid:

* patchy watercolor
* dense edge-to-edge scenery
* fully closed vignette shapes
* heavy black outlines
* extra characters
* extra props
* anatomy drift
* missing or extra wings
* missing or extra limbs
* blended facial traits between characters
* photorealism
* CGI
* glossy 3D
* anime styling
* polished vector line art
* anything that advances the story beyond this exact beat

**Priority order: character recognition first, story action second, atmosphere third, detail last.**
