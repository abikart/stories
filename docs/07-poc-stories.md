# POC stories — page-by-page briefs

Three stories, chosen to exercise every engine feature between them. Text is a starting draft — the decodability linter is the authority; adjust words freely but keep the arcs and cue moments. All narration slow and warm; all pages start dormant.

---

## 1. The Fox on the Box — Level 1, coded scene (the flagship)

Scope: CVC with `s a t p i n m d g o c k e u r h b f l x j`; sight words `the, is, and, a`. One SVG scene used across all pages (continuous world, camera nudges per page — the floema trick of one space explored across moments). Fox is poke-able after page wake (tail flick).

| Pg | Text (draft) | Scene beat (cues) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The fox sat on the box. | Fox settles onto box (`sit` @ "sat") |
| 2 | The box is big. | Camera pulls back revealing box size (`reveal` @ "big") |
| 3 | A bug is on the box. | Bug crawls up the side (`crawl` @ "bug") |
| 4 | The fox met the bug. | Fox leans down nose-to-nose (`lean` @ "met") |
| 5 | The bug hops on the fox! | Bug leaps to fox's nose (`hop` @ "hops") |
| 6 | The fox hops on the box! | Fox startle-jumps onto box (`jump` @ "hops", `land` @ "box") |
| 7 | The box tips! | Box wobbles and tilts (`wobble` @ "tips") |
| 8 | The fox and the bug sit in the box. | Both peek out of tipped box, content (`peek` @ "sit"); full wake = fireflies + evening ambience |

Sticker: fox and bug in the box.

## 2. The Ship in the Rain — Level 2, frames scenes (the pipeline proof)

Scope: level 1 graphemes + `sh ch th ai ee`; sight words `the, is, on, no`. Every page is a procedurally generated frame sequence (rain streaks, layered sea parallax, drifting ship, sun rays — all Canvas-achievable). Exercises digraph/vowel-team highlighting (`sh`, `ai`, `ee`).

| Pg | Text (draft) | Frame sequence beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The ship is in the rain. | Rain intensifies, ship rocks gently |
| 2 | Drip, drip, drip on the deck. | Three drips hit the deck in rhythm (`drip`×3) |
| 3 | A fish leaps up. Splish! | Fish arcs over the bow (`leap` @ "leaps", `splash` @ "Splish") |
| 4 | The rain stops. No drips. | Rain thins to nothing, clouds part |
| 5 | The sun is up. The ship sails on. | Sun rays sweep, sea sparkles, ship sails toward horizon |

Sticker: the ship under a rainbow.

## 3. Pop! Pop! Pop! — Level 1, coded scene (pure delight)

Scope: CVC as story 1; sight words `the, a`. A popcorn pot: each read word in the climax page pops one kernel — the tightest possible word→world feedback. Popped corn accumulates across pages (scene state persists). After final wake, tapping anywhere pops bonus kernels.

| Pg | Text (draft) | Scene beat |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pip has a pot. | Pip (round little character) pats the pot (`pat` @ "pot") |
| 2 | The pot is hot. | Heat shimmer, pot glows (`heat` @ "hot") |
| 3 | It pops! Pop! Pop! Pop! | One kernel pops **per word** (`pop` ×4) |
| 4 | Pop, pop, pop — ten big pops! | Popcorn fountain builds word-by-word (`pop`×3, `fountain` @ "pops") |
| 5 | Pip sits in the pops. | Pip flops happily into the popcorn pile (`flop` @ "sits") |

Sticker: Pip buried in popcorn.
