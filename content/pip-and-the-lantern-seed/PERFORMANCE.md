# Performance direction

## Voice

A warm, captivating grown-up storyteller speaking to one child at bedtime.
Natural American English, intimate rather than theatrical, unhurried but never
instructional. Preserve the musical rhythm of short sentences and let the
quiet beats breathe. Pip is earnest and small; Glow is gentle and lightly
playful. Dialogue is characterized through inflection, not cartoon voices.

## Emotional arc

1. **Wonder with a tiny wrong note** — Bramble Hollow glows, but Pip's home
   does not.
2. **Tender concern into playful release** — the trapped firefly and the “Pop!”
   should lift the energy.
3. **Vulnerability into companionship** — honor the pause after Pip admits she
   is lost.
4. **Growing momentum** — the journey phrase should feel gently rhythmic.
5. **Warm bloom** — allow space around “The lantern bloomed.”
6. **Quiet belonging** — finish like the last page of a beloved picture book,
   not like a moral being explained.

## Generation notes

- Generate the entire story as one performance so breath, pacing, and character
  inflection remain coherent.
- Bracketed direction in `performance-source.json` is non-spoken Eleven v3
  direction. The clean manuscript is the alignment authority.
- Do not add laughs, sighs, or other non-scripted vocal events; they make exact
  alignment and repeated listening less predictable.
