# Visual direction — Pip and the Lantern Seed

## World and medium

An original hand-painted storybook world: opaque gouache and translucent
watercolor on softly textured paper, with simplified rounded silhouettes,
cinematic twilight color, and delicate pencil edges. Bramble Hollow is made of
small cottages tucked into roots and green hills. The palette moves from deep
indigo and blue-green through plum dusk into butter-yellow lantern light.

This is not an imitation of any named studio, artist, film, or published book.
Avoid photorealism, 3D-rendered surfaces, anime conventions, text, captions,
logos, frames, and UI.

## Cast continuity

- **Pip:** a small deep-blue moth child, round and earnest, with two soft
  crescent-patterned wings, short antennae, tiny hands, a butter-yellow knitted
  scarf, and a cross-body acorn-lantern satchel. Pip's eyes are warm dark ovals,
  never oversized or glossy.
- **Glow:** a tiny golden firefly with a teardrop-shaped body, two transparent
  wings, simple dot eyes, and a warm diffuse halo. Glow is much smaller than
  Pip and reads as a light first, character second.
- **Lantern:** an acorn-shaped brass-and-glass hand lantern with a leaf-shaped
  handle. It is dark in early scenes and blooms with warm light near the end.

## Composition contract

- One 16:9 landscape master for every visual state.
- Keep all faces, gestures, the lantern, Glow, and the hero path inside the
  centered 4:3 action-safe region (the middle 75% of frame width).
- The outer 12.5% on each side contains atmosphere only and may crop on phone.
- Locked or nearly locked camera. Motion is living-illustration motion, not
  cinematic camera movement.
- No baked text. HTML overlays own all words and dialogue.

## Golden scene states

1. **The cold lantern — idle loop.** Moonrise outside Pip's root cottage.
   Distant Bramble Hollow doors glow; Pip's acorn lantern remains dark. Pip
   stands just left of center, quietly trying the lantern. Ferns and scarf move
   in a faint breeze; distant windows flicker. Locked camera, seamless calm loop.
2. **The tiny light — action.** Beneath broad ferns, Pip gently frees Glow from
   one silver spider thread. Pip pulls twice; the thread releases; Glow floats
   free and settles beside Pip. Locked camera, clear beginning and resolved end
   frame; no visible spider.
3. **The blue dark — idle loop.** The forest path is lost in blue twilight.
   Pip waits with the dark lantern in the left-center target area; Glow hovers in
   the right-center start area. Sleepy mushrooms, a narrow creek, and one leaning
   pine form a readable curved route between them. Only breath, leaves, water,
   and Glow's halo move.
4. **The blue dark — resolve loop.** Exact same composition as state 3. A warm
   dotted light trail now travels from Glow along the curved route to Pip's
   lantern; mushrooms and creek edges answer with a faint glow. Preserve exact
   character scale and camera for a clean crossfade.
5. **The lantern blooms — living illustration.** At Pip's doorstep, Glow circles
   the acorn lantern while a golden spark slips inside. The lantern opens into a
   soft flower of light; Pip's expression shifts from wonder to delight. Locked
   camera; the final state may hold and breathe.
6. **The window — closing loop.** Wide peaceful Bramble Hollow night. Pip is a
   small silhouette inside the cottage, leaving one warm window glowing toward
   a winding dark path. Fireflies drift, grasses breathe, and the window remains
   the emotional focal point. Quiet, safe, last-page feeling.

## Motion and output

- 720p or better, 16:9, six seconds where supported.
- No generated audio; the web runtime supplies the authored mix.
- Favor stable faces and silhouettes over complex action.
- Keep idle/resolve states loopable; the tiny-light action may play once and
  hold its final frame.
