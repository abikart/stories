# Pip and the Lantern Seed

In Bramble Hollow, every door glowed before moonrise. Every door but Pip's.
Pip was the smallest lantern keeper, and tonight her lantern would not wake.
She polished the glass. She whispered, “Please?” Nothing.

Then a tiny light blinked beneath the ferns. It was Glow, a firefly caught in a
silver spider thread. “Hold still,” said Pip. She tugged once. Twice. Pop! Glow
floated free.

But the path home had vanished into the blue dark. Pip's wings trembled. “I
don't know the way.” Glow hovered beside her. “Then we will find it together.”
Past the sleepy mushrooms, across the whispering creek, and under the leaning
pine they went. Pip followed the little warm light.

At her doorstep, Glow circled the cold lantern. One golden spark slipped
inside. The lantern bloomed. “You woke it!” Pip cheered. Glow shook his head.
“We did.”

From that night on, Pip left one window glowing for anyone who had lost the
way.
