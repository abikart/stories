## b01 — bell-tree-dawn

Inputs: approved Fern/Pipkin identity, seed-bell sheet, bell-tree location, and
`docs/universe/fern-and-pip.png`.

```text

Asset type: native 4:3 children's story keyframe for ages 5–7
Story beat: Establish the remembered Lanternleaf morning ritual. Fern and
Pipkin stand together beneath the old bell tree as the three canonical seed

quiet delight; the village is still just about to wake.
Reference roles: The accepted Fern-and-Pipkin identity image controls exact
character anatomy, proportions, palette, Fern's dusty-teal satchel, Pipkin's
gold-tipped antennae, and exactly four warm-gold wings. The seed-bell sheet
controls the exact three bell shapes, dusty-blue, berry-pink, and warm-ochre
ribbon colors, beads, and fasteners. The approved bell-tree location controls
the same old tree, root-cottage relationship, cozy low viewpoint, and organic
white vignette only; do not copy its wet one-high-knot state. The Fern-and-
Pipkin universe image controls only lived-in village scale and recognizable
useful details.
Location: The same bell tree beside two root cottages with rounded CLOSED
shutters, quiet oven vents with NO puff yet, hooks with morning tools, potted
bellflowers, varied ferns, and a pale path toward nearby neighbors. No stream or
bridge.
Characters: Fern stands upright at lower left with her unchanged dusty-teal
satchel, one paw resting near its strap. Pipkin hovers beside Fern at lower-
middle with exactly four visible wings. Both look upward, calm and happy,
listening to the familiar opening chime.
Continuity now: bells are ringing-memory; ribbons are dry, orderly, separate,
and fully open with NO knots or tangles; Fern and Pipkin are beside each other;

enclosed fill; clearer on foreground silhouettes, lighter in the environment,
absent in distant washes. Never one universal ink color.

shadows, while preserving the white matte.
Must preserve: exactly one Fern, one Pipkin, Fern upright with the same satchel,
Pipkin with exactly four wings, exactly three separate canonical bells, dry
orderly open ribbons, closed shutters, quiet oven vents, no high knot, no rain,
no premature village waking.

wind gust, drawn sound words, paper/canvas texture, beige or gray matte, full-

stippling, sketch lines, repeated surface marks, digital glow, text, border,
watermark, vector finish, glossy 3D, anime, or photorealism.
```
