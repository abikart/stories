# Fern and the Silent Seed Bells — motion generation prompts

Exact Grok Imagine motion prompts are recorded here before submission. Accepted
source keyframes remain authoritative. Provider IDs, technical hashes, delivery
paths, and acceptance decisions live in `motion-production.json`.

## Motion contract

- Native 4:3 image-to-video only; never crop or reframe the accepted keyframe.
- Locked camera, no cuts, zoom, pan, parallax invention, or hard rectangular
  background.
- Preserve the organic pure-white matte and every continuity-bearing character,
  prop, ribbon, cottage, and path.
- Stable states are restrained living loops whose first and last frames match.
- Transformations are one-shot actions that occur once and settle; they never
  reset or replay inside the clip.
- No text, captions, logos, watermarks, drawn sound marks, digital glow, extra
  characters, duplicate anatomy, or style drift.
