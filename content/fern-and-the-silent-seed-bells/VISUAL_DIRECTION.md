## Format and matte

- Generate every keyframe natively at 4:3; do not request a hidden 16:9 frame.
- Keep the full action, high knot, path, and interaction regions readable in the
  one 4:3 frame.
- Use a solid `#FFFFFF` matte. Vignette edges must dissolve organically without
  beige paper, grain, alpha dependence, or a visible rectangular source canvas.
- Use locally color-matched contours, not one brown or black outline.
- Make the bell-tree locations rich through readable seed pods, ribbons, tools,
  windows, plants, and signs of morning work—not through surface texture.

## Canonical story references to generate first

1. **Character turnaround on white:** Fern and Pipkin front, three-quarter, and
   side scale; exact satchel, antennae, and four-wing anatomy.
2. **Seed-bell prop sheet:** silent, partially freed, ready, and ringing states;
   consistent pod shapes, ribbons, fasteners, and color order.
3. **Bell-tree location reference:** low branches, thorny canopy, high knot,
   adjacent root cottages, shutters, oven vent, and interaction path geometry.

Do not generate continuity-critical motion until these three references pass.

## Motion grammar

- Living holds: leaf tips, breathing, hanging cords, distant shutters, light
  steam, and restrained watercolor movement that never shifts the matte edge.
- One-shots: loosening lower knots, opening the final knot, breeze arrival,
  first restored bell ring, and village waking.
- Hero interaction: child-guided Pipkin flight from Fern's traced path to the
  high knot. The underlying scene remains stable; the resolved media settles
  with Pipkin beside the still-closed knot.
- Never loop knot opening, breeze arrival, bell restoration, or village waking.

## Acceptance gates

- Fern remains upright with the dusty-teal satchel; Pipkin retains exactly four
  warm-gold wings and gold-tipped antennae.
- The bells are ringing normally only in the opening memory and after `u13`.
- The high knot remains closed through the interaction result and opens once at
  `u11`; no earlier frame may show a loose upper ribbon.
- The restored breeze and communal morning appear only after the knot opens.
- Every narration-bearing unit receives a semantically matching visual state;
  narrated motion is never covered by an unrelated living loop.
- Inspect start, midpoint, and end of each video for anatomy, prop, action, matte,
  and final-hold continuity.
